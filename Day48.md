docs(journal): Day 48 – ABTALKS Claude AI 60 Days Challenge

Day 48 focused on designing a professional comparison and decision-support experience around SafeSipAi.

Highlights:
- Explored structured decision-making using measurable evaluation criteria.
- Planned an interactive comparison tool with dynamic weighting and live ranking.
- Designed transparent research methodology with visible citations and source tracking.
- Included support for loading states, empty states, responsive layouts, and accessibility.
- Added plans for a collapsible "How this was researched" section to improve trust.
- Emphasized separating verified facts from estimates or placeholders.

What I learnt:
- Good decision tools should prioritize transparency over flashy scores.
- Every metric should be backed by verifiable sources instead of assumptions.
- Allowing users to adjust criterion weights makes recommendations more personal and useful.
- Research provenance is just as important as the final ranking.
