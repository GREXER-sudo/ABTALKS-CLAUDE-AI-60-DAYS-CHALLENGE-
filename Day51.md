Day 51 — Product Discovery & Sprint Planning

AB Talks Claude AI 60 Days Challenge

Today marked the transition from learning AI prompting into thinking like a real product builder.

Instead of immediately jumping into code, I focused on understanding why successful software projects begin with planning. A strong product isn't built by writing code first—it's built by defining the right problem, understanding users, and keeping the scope realistic.

Key Learnings

- Requirements gathering is more important than choosing a tech stack.
- Good products solve one problem extremely well instead of trying to solve everything.
- Scope creep is one of the biggest reasons projects fail, especially under tight deadlines.
- A Product Requirements Document (PRD) becomes the single source of truth for development.
- An implementation blueprint removes uncertainty by breaking the project into achievable daily milestones.
- Every feature should directly support the core value proposition.
- Building a polished MVP is far more valuable than building an unfinished ambitious project.
